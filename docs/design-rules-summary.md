# Vibes Fund: Not AI Design Rules (summary)

Full doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/L5d6qNsrQ8k7QcgBADKfDm

Every Vibes Fund design (site, Live card, letters, factsheets, video) follows these rules. Break one only on purpose, with a written reason.

## Rules
- Start from 3 to 5 real reference artifacts of the genre. A style description is not a reference.
- Type: one text family, at most one display face. Never Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Lato, system fonts, Space Grotesk, Fraunces, IBM Plex, JetBrains Mono. No script fonts as signatures. Six type sizes, display at least 3x body, tabular figures in tables.
- Color: one ink, one paper, one accent with a single job. No gradients, glows, blobs. No cream plus serif plus orange.
- Layout: 12 column grid used asymmetrically. One dominant element per screen. Group with whitespace and rules, not boxes. Spacing scale 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128.
- Components: no cards by default, radius 0 except round subjects, no shadows, no eyebrows, dot separated lists, pill badges or decorative dots.
- Imagery: one style per direction with written rules. No clip art made of circles and ellipses. Prefer type, texture, real photos or a labeled placeholder.
- Copy: specific to this fund, at most one joke per section, no repeated formulas, no "X. Real Y." slogans.
- Data: use the genre's conventions (highlights table, schedule of investments, footnotes). Label sample data once.
- Motion: one moment per page at most, none for reduced motion.

## Direction A v2: Mood Ring Capital
1970s two color annual report: black ink plus one spot color, which is the day's mood ink. Chivo only. Paper #F4F3EE, ink #161513. Mood inks: Doom #2B2B2B, Nervous #C98A1B, Unbothered #4F8A5B, Optimistic #2F6FA8, Euphoric #7B4FA8, used for large shapes only. Hairlines and one 8 px heavy rule.
- Halftone everywhere (William, Nov 5): every place a mood color appears (ring, logo, chips, swatches, chart bars, table markers, buttons) is printed with the paper colored dot screen, never flat. Large areas use the 7 px screen rotated 15 degrees; small marks (16 to 24 px) use a 4 to 5 px screen so the dots stay legible. Small mood markers are at least 16 px.

## Direction B v2: Sentimental Value Partners
One engraved certificate, then a bound ledger. Old Standard TT only. Paper #F1EEE6, ink #1C1B19, banknote green #2E5A44 for engraved borders only. Line engraving for imagery. Seal as blind emboss only.

## Characters
- The Chairman's house portrait is claude/chairman-portrait.svg: classic mid-century gag-cartoon ink. He is small and sits in an enormous tufted wingback chair (chair and carnation in the day's mood ink, screened) with his feet dangling, holding a teacup by its handle above its saucer. Reuse this drawing; do not redraw him from scratch.

## Illustration process (William, Nov 5)
Do not patch drawings by editing coordinates and judging from code. For any illustration:
1. Plan the pose and composition first.
2. Build hard parts (hands, props) as large studies, look at them, then place them.
3. Render every round at enlarged size and at real page size, and have a fresh reviewer agent critique the renders against the brief and a fixed checklist.
4. Fix and repeat; publish only after the review passes.
Ask William for reference images when a part depends on real anatomy or objects; the work environment cannot load images from the web.

## Pre ship checklist
Zoom to 25%; audit radii, shadows, gaps and sizes; count typefaces; remove eyebrows, separators, pills; justify every box; reread copy for reuse and repeated formulas; check illustration style; swap the fund name test; check every mood color is screened; contrast 4.5:1; final human pass.
